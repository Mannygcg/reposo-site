Common questions

My apps unlocked by themselves on iPhone

Turning off Screen Time access for Reposo in Settings lifts every block. iOS doesn't let any app prevent this. Open Reposo and allow access again.

Tapping a tag does nothing on iPhone

iOS only reads tags while Reposo is open. Tap Tap tag in Reposo, or create a Shortcuts automation: Personal Automation → NFC → Start Reposo.

Blocking stopped on Android

Reinstalling or some battery savers turn off Reposo's accessibility service. Open Reposo; it will show you how to turn it back on.

Which NFC tags work?

Stickers, cards and keychains (NTAG213/215/216 are a good choice). Phones, watches and bank cards change their ID and can't be used.
