Privacy policy

Reposo for iPhone and Android · Effective November 5, 2026

In short

Reposo collects nothing. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and we run no servers. Everything Reposo knows stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your modes and the apps, categories and websites in them
  • Your tags, QR codes and schedules
  • Your lock history, streaks, catches, badges and fisher
  • On Android, a log of what the blocker did, and the notifications held while you were locked so you can read them afterwards

This data is deleted when you delete the app. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Permissions and why

  • Screen Time (iPhone): to block the apps and websites you choose. Apple only gives Reposo anonymous tokens for them, not your usage.
  • Accessibility service (Android): to see which app or website is open so a blocked one can be covered. This is checked on the phone and never recorded beyond the local block log.
  • Notification access (Android): to hide notifications from blocked apps and show them to you when the lock ends.
  • Usage access (Android): to suggest your most used apps during setup.
  • NFC and camera: to read your tags and scan QR codes. No photos are taken or saved.

The Android app doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

Tips

If you leave a tip, the payment is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We never see your payment details.

Children

Reposo collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
